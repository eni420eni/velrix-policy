# Velrix Community Guidelines & Moderation Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Velrix is a private, end-to-end encrypted messenger. These rules explain what is not allowed, what you can do about abuse, and, honestly, what we can and cannot do about it.

## 1. How Velrix moderation works (and its limits)

- Messages, calls and media are end-to-end encrypted and travel peer-to-peer over Tor. **Velrix cannot read, scan, or remove your content**, and we run no central message server that could do so.
- Because of this, moderation is **user-driven**: you decide who can contact you, and you can report people to us.
- We cannot delete accounts or ban identities network-wide. The tools that exist are the ones on your own device: **block** and **report**.

## 2. What is not allowed

You may not use Velrix to:

1. Harass, threaten, stalk, intimidate or bully others.
2. Send hateful content targeting people for race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or nationality.
3. Share sexual content involving minors, or any sexual content without the consent of everyone involved. Content that sexualises minors is reported to the authorities where required by law.
4. Promote or incite violence, terrorism or self-harm.
5. Send spam, scams, phishing or unsolicited bulk messages.
6. Impersonate another person or organisation.
7. Share someone's private information without consent (doxxing) or intimate images without consent.
8. Distribute malware or attempt to attack other users or the app.
9. Break the law, or help others do so.
10. Evade a block by creating new profiles to contact someone who has blocked you.

## 3. Your tools

### Block (permanent)
In any chat: attachment (+) menu → **Block user**.
- The person can no longer contact you and their chat is deleted from your phone.
- The block is stored outside your profile and survives profile deletion and re-registration.
- It is designed to also cover new profiles created by the same person.
- You can review and undo blocks in Profile → **Blocked Users**. The list shows the name the person had when you blocked them and, if they changed it, their newer name.

### Report
In any chat: attachment (+) menu → **Report user**. Pick a reason and your email app opens a draft to our support address. Nothing is sent until you press send.
The draft contains the reported name, technical identifiers of the reported user and the time. **No message content is attached automatically.** Add a description of what happened (names, dates, what was said) so we can consider it. Note that your email address will be visible to us.

## 4. What we do with reports

- We review reports in good faith, aiming to respond within 7 days where possible.
- Because we cannot verify encrypted content, we rely on what you provide. We may ask for more detail.
- Possible outcomes: no action, advice on using block, or, for serious cases, cooperation with law enforcement when legally required or when there is a risk to someone's safety.
- Reports from repeat or malicious submitters (for example, false reports to harass someone) may be disregarded.
- Reports are used only to handle the case and are kept no longer than needed. We do not sell or share them except as stated here.

## 5. Continued harassment

No tool can guarantee that a determined abuser is stopped. If someone keeps contacting you after being blocked, report them and include what happened so we can look into it.

**If the harassment is serious, threatening, or is affecting your wellbeing, please contact the police.** Harassment, threats, stalking and blackmail are crimes in most countries. Save evidence before blocking, because blocking deletes the chat from your phone. Screenshots are disabled inside Velrix to protect your privacy, so write down the person's name, the dates and times, and what was said, or photograph the screen with another device, and give this to the police. Velrix cannot identify or locate users, but we will cooperate with lawful requests where we are able.

## 6. Serious and urgent situations

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. Velrix cannot trace or locate users.

## 7. Appeals

If you were blocked and believe it was a mistake, contact the person directly through another channel; blocks are a personal choice of each user and Velrix cannot lift them for you.

## 8. Changes

We may update these guidelines. The date above shows the latest version. Continuing to use Velrix means you accept the current version.
